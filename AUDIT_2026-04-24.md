## Overall rating — **RED. Do not ship as-is.**

- The site's public claim (README, docs/CONTENT_SOURCES.md) is that every ATC-attributed passage is 100% verbatim.
- That claim is **not true as currently implemented**. Specifically:
  - The `FEATURES` array (line 2479) contains ~18 developer-written descriptions rendered inside `<blockquote class="verb">` tags with "Source: verbatim" footers. These are **fabrications attributed to ATC**.
  - All 5 testimonials in `TESTIMONIALS` (line 1942) are condensed/edited and in two cases include invented phrasing.
  - FAQ 14 (care for used camper) is missing entire paragraphs from ATC's answer; additional phrases are deleted and an em-dash is inserted where ATC did not use one.
  - Hero `<h1>` uses "Aluminum" (American spelling) while ATC consistently writes "Aluminium" (British). Same phrase elsewhere in the file correctly preserves "Aluminium," so the H1 is a deviation, not the convention.
  - Multiple MODELS entries have inserted punctuation, re-typed quote marks, or silently-corrected ATC typos (e.g. Shell "weight" → "weigh").
- Prices, contact info, social links, FAQs 1–6 / 8–13 / 15–17, and 5 of 7 MODEL spec summaries are solid and verbatim.

**To ship to ATC honestly, the two choices are:**
1. Replace every altered/fabricated passage with ATC's exact wording, or
2. Remove the "verbatim" framing from anywhere the text isn't actually verbatim (no `<blockquote class="verb">`, no "Source: verbatim" footer), and let the developer-written copy stand as descriptive summary.

Option 2 is faster. Option 1 is stronger as a sale asset. Either is acceptable; the current state is not.
